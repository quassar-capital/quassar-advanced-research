# Quassar Advanced Research

**Advanced Scientific Research | Computational Engineering | Applied Mathematics | Artificial Intelligence**

## Overview

Quassar Advanced Research is an interdisciplinary scientific research and computational engineering initiative within Quassar Capital. The repository is intended to support the development, evaluation, and documentation of numerical methods, simulation frameworks, and research software across selected areas of science and engineering.

Its work will emphasize mathematical rigor, physical consistency, numerical reliability, and reproducible results. Research activities will be distinguished from operational systems, commercial products, and the firm's proprietary investment infrastructure.

## Research Domains

### Aerospace Engineering

Computational approaches to flight mechanics, aerospace dynamics, structural behavior, and the analysis of physical systems operating under complex environmental conditions.

### Computational Aerodynamics

Numerical investigation of fluid motion, turbulence, heat transfer, and aerodynamic phenomena, including the verification and validation of computational fluid dynamics methods.

### Artificial Intelligence & Scientific Computing

Machine learning, numerical optimization, scientific machine learning, and computational methods for analyzing complex datasets and physical processes.

### Applied Mathematics & Physics

Differential equations, numerical linear algebra, dynamical systems, uncertainty quantification, and mathematical models of physical phenomena.

### Advanced Materials & Structural Mechanics

Computational methods for examining material behavior, deformation, stress, thermal effects, and structural response under defined conditions.

### Dynamics & Fundamental Defense-Related Research

Foundational research in mechanics, trajectory mathematics, sensing, and physical simulation, limited to academic, non-operational applications. The repository is not intended to provide weapon design, targeting capabilities, or operational military systems.

## Engineering Methodology

Research and software development will be guided by the following principles:

- **Scientific validity:** Models grounded in clearly stated mathematical assumptions and established physical principles.
- **Numerical verification:** Systematic evaluation of convergence, stability, accuracy, and computational error.
- **Experimental validation:** Comparison with suitable reference solutions, published benchmarks, or independently verifiable observations when available.
- **Reproducibility:** Documented environments, controlled dependencies, deterministic testing where practical, and traceable computational results.
- **Software quality:** Modular architecture, automated testing, code review, and appropriate performance assessment.
- **Responsible research:** Technical review of safety, security, intellectual property, and potential dual-use implications before public release.

## Technology & Computational Infrastructure

The technical stack will be selected according to the mathematical and computational requirements of each project. Potential technologies include Python, C++, Rust, Julia, and GPU-accelerated computing where justified by research needs.

Supporting practices may include version control, continuous integration, numerical regression testing, scientific documentation, benchmark evaluation, and reproducible computational environments.

Technology selection does not imply that any particular framework, implementation, or computing infrastructure is currently operational.

## Scope of Public Materials

The repository is currently private. Any future public release will be subject to scientific, security, intellectual property, and institutional review.

Approved materials may include general-purpose numerical methods, academic simulations, nonproprietary research software, and educational examples. Confidential research, sensitive technical information, proprietary systems, and materials presenting unacceptable dual-use risks will remain outside the public scope.
